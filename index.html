The School was established in May 1983 with the intention of having an exclusive institution for excelling in Academics, co-curricular activities and overall development of the children and ever since it has grown very rapidly. The aim of the School is not only to make learning pleasurable but also educating the children to become good citizens. To inculcate a high sense of responsibility, duty, integrity and spirit of co-operation. A sound Educational setup strongly recommends positive discipline and personality development in every child. The School strives to provide a nurturing and stimulating environment where every child is encouraged to discover their potential, develop confidence and embrace lifelong learning. The School is run by the founder and the Principal Mrs. Rehana Khan with the help of the School Board of Directors and our efficient Teachers. Our Teachers in addition to imparting Education through prescribed curriculum also cater many innovative Programs in order to inculcate in the young minds the fundamental values of our cultural heritage, we are always in search of new ideas and innovation.
